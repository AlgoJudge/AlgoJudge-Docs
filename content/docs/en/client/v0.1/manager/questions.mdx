---
title: Questions and announcements
description: Answering one person, publishing the answer to everyone, and announcing.
---

The area lists questions and announcements across every activity you manage.
Filter by activity, by series inside it, by kind, or by a search on the topic,
the body or the author — and there is a **waiting for an answer** switch for the
one list that matters during a contest. The filters are in the address, so the
list you are working from is a link.

## Answering and publishing are two acts

**An answer stays private until you publish it.** Most answers concern one team's
submission and would tell everybody else nothing; the few that are worth
publishing become the FAQ a contest needs.

The answer dialog carries one switch — **publish to every participant** — so
answering and publishing can be done in a single step where you already know the
answer is general. Leave it off when the answer concerns only this person.

An answer can be edited afterwards, and a published question can be unpublished.

**An unanswered question cannot be published**: showing everyone the doubt
without the answer helps nobody, and the control is refused rather than failing
after the click.

Answering is `question:answer`; publishing is `question:publish`. Both are in the
shipped `manager` template, and an installation may hand out one without the
other.

## Announcements

**New announcement** needs an activity chosen in the filter above it — an
announcement belongs to one. It takes a topic, a body, and optionally a series;
empty means the whole activity.

**An announcement is published the moment it is created.** There is no draft
state for one, and the dialog says so before you save. It can be deleted
afterwards; a question cannot.

A published item carries a read count, which is how you find out whether the
thing you announced was seen.

## Turning the whole thing off

Questions and announcements are a **module** on the activity's settings tab.
Disabled, it leaves the participant's sidebar entirely rather than showing an
empty screen.
