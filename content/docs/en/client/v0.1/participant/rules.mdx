---
title: Rules
description: The regulations, where the organiser published any.
---

**Rules** in the navigation is the activity's regulations, written by whoever
runs it.

**It is there only when a document exists.** Whether there are rules is whether
somebody published them, not a switch beside them that could be on over nothing.
Where none were published there is no entry and nothing links to the page.

Two things follow:

- The entry is shown **whether or not you are enrolled**. Rules are one of the
  few things somebody outside the activity may read, and they are what the
  enrolment form asks you to accept.
- Where the activity publishes rules, the enrolment form carries **I have read
  and accept the rules**, required, and clicking it opens the document over the
  form rather than taking you away from it.

The rules are written in the same format as a problem statement, and are
published per language where the organiser wrote more than one. You get the one
matching your interface language, falling back to what was written first.

If you reach the page from an old bookmark and the rules have since been
withdrawn, it says *This activity publishes no rules.*
