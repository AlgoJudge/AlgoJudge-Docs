title: The manager panel

The panel lives at `/manager`. Its landing screen lists what you may open and
nothing else: *everything you may administer here — what you may not is not
listed*.

That is not decoration. The sidebar, the landing screen and the guard on every
route all read one table, so a card cannot offer you a screen that then answers
403, and a screen you can reach cannot be missing from the menu.

## The thirteen areas

Holding **any one** of the permissions in the last column opens the area.

| Area | What it is for | Any one of these admits |
|---|---|---|
| [Users](/en/client/manager/enrolment) | Accounts, temporary logins in bulk, blocking and notes. | `user:read:all` |
| [Grants](/en/client/manager/permissions) | Who may do what, in the system and in each activity. | `grant:read:all` |
| [Roles](/en/client/manager/permissions) | The sets a grant points at. Editing one reaches everybody holding it. | `role:read`, `grant:update` |
| [Problems](/en/client/manager/problems) | The library: statements, attachments, packages and versions. | `problem:read:own`, `problem:read:all` |
| [Activities](/en/client/manager/activities) | Contests and courses: series, assignments, times and enrolment. | `activity:create`, `activity:update`, `activity:archive` |
| [Submissions](/en/client/manager/submissions) | Every submission, its source, its attempts and a rejudge. | `submission:read:all` |
| [Questions and announcements](/en/client/manager/questions) | Answering, publishing an answer to everyone, announcing. | `question:read:all`, `question:answer` |
| [Printouts](/en/client/manager/printouts) | The queue of source somebody asked to have on paper. | `printout:manage` |
| [Runners](/en/client/manager/runners) | The machines that evaluate: approval, tags and their reports. | `runner:read` |
| [Instance](/en/client/manager/instance) | What this installation is called, the mark it shows, and the documents it publishes. | `instance:update` |
| [External logins](/en/client/manager/logins) | Identity providers, what a claim from one grants, and the accounts asked to go. | `provider:manage` |
| [LTI platforms](/en/client/manager/logins) | Course platforms that may open activities for their students. | `provider:manage` |
| [External content](/en/client/manager/external-content) | Where this installation may fetch documents from, and whether it may at all. | `instance:update` |

Hold none of them and the panel has nothing in it for you: neither the entry in
the navigation nor the landing screen appears.

## What "you hold it" means here

An area is offered when you hold its permission **anywhere** — in one activity
is enough. Somebody who runs a single course still needs the panel that course
lives in.

The screens themselves are stricter. Inside an activity, what you may hand out
is what you hold *in that activity*, which is not the same set as your rights
across the installation. See [Permissions and
grants](/en/client/manager/permissions).

## Where the manager's job stops

Six of the thirteen areas are about the installation rather than about any one
activity: Users, Runners, Instance, External logins, LTI platforms and External
content. Between them they need four permissions — `user:read:all`,
`runner:read`, `instance:update` and `provider:manage` — and **none of the four
is in the shipped `manager` role**. Running an activity is not running the
installation it lives in.

**Roles used to be a seventh, and is not any more.** `role:manage` is scoped:
held in an activity grant it writes that activity's roles and nothing else, so a
manager may correct what their own participants hold without reaching the
installation's roles at all.

**Printouts is the one written to be handed out on its own.** A grant carrying
`printout:manage` and nothing else opens that area and refuses every other one,
which is how somebody standing at a printer is given that job and no other. The
cost is stated on its page: the key makes them staff in that activity, and staff
are not in its ranking.

Beyond even those is a third reader. Installing AlgoJudge, backing it up,
updating it and getting it back after a failure is not done from this panel at
all; it is [Install and operate](/en/install).
