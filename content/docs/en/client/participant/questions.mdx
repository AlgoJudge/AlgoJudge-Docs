title: Questions and announcements
description: Asking about the activity, one round or one problem, and reading what the organisers publish.
---

**Questions and announcements** is one list holding two kinds of thing, told
apart by a badge:

- a **Question**, asked by a participant,
- an **Announcement**, published by the organisers.

The entry is only there where the organiser switched the module on. A course
legitimately has none.

## Reading

The table carries **Topic**, **Author**, **Series**, **Problem** and **Date**.
Rows you have not read are bold; a reply icon marks a question that has been
answered.

The list arrives sorted by **Date**, newest first. Clicking **Series**,
**Problem** or **Date** sorts by it, descending; clicking the column already
sorted flips the direction. Topic and author are deliberately not sortable —
nobody looks for a question by the first letter of its subject.

Filter by a search over the topic, by kind, by series and by problem. Filtering
and paging both happen on the Server, ten rows to a page.

Clicking a row opens the whole thing: the body, and the answer underneath with
who wrote it and when. An unanswered question says *Not answered yet*.

**Read state is yours and lives on the Server**, so a question you opened on one
machine is read on the next.

## Asking

**Ask question** opens a form. The first thing it asks is **what the question is
about**, because that decides who ends up reading it:

| Scope | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Whole activity** | anything general |
| **Series** | one round, week or class |
| **Problem** | one problem |

Only **open** rounds, and the problems inside them, can be chosen. A problem in a
round that has not started is not something you can have read, so it is not
something you can ask about — a general question still works.

Then a **Topic** and the question itself. Both are required.

You cannot post an announcement. That is a manager's act, and the form does not
offer it.

## Staying current

An answer, a question published to everybody, and a new announcement all arrive
while the page is open. You do not have to reload to see them.
