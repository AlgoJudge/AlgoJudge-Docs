title: Your account

**My account** is in the menu under your name, top right. Three tabs: **Profile**,
**Password** and **My data**.

An account that came from an identity provider carries an **SSO** badge beside
the title and the notice *This account is managed by the identity provider. Your
name, login, address and password are changed there.* Both the profile fields and
the password tab are disabled for it. The provider owns the identity.

## Profile

**First name** and **Last name** are optional. **Username** is required — it is
what you sign in as, and **it is what other people see when you have given no
name**, the ranking included.

Changing your **Email** makes the address unconfirmed again.

## Password

Your current password, then the new one twice. **At least 12 characters.**

There is no self-service reset anywhere in the product. A forgotten password is
reissued by an administrator.

## My data

### Export

**Download my data** saves everything held about you as a file you keep.

### Deleting the account

<Callout type="warn" title="Immediate and irreversible">
Your submissions and results **stay**, under an identifier that no longer names
you.
</Callout>

What the screen offers depends on where your account lives.

**A local account.** Type `DELETE` in the confirmation box, give your password,
and press **Delete the account**. The button stays disabled until both are
right.

**An account from a provider.** The act is a different one and is named
differently: **Delete my account** removes your link to the identity provider,
and a dialog asks once before it happens. If that link is the only way you sign
in, the account is emptied — immediately, on the same terms as above.

**Your account at the provider stays.** Where the provider publishes a way to
delete it, the screen offers that as a second, separate link. Doing only the
first leaves you still signed in at the provider and able to walk straight back
in.
